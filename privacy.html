Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Blabacus collects no data. Free. Private. On-device. Your conversations never leave your Mac.

  • Audio from the microphone is analyzed in memory, on your Mac, only while you have pressed Start. It is never recorded, stored, or transmitted.
  • Voiceprints: if you choose to remember a speaker's voice, Blabacus stores their name and a voiceprint (a list of numbers describing the voice, not a recording) in the app's private container on your Mac. You can rename or delete them anytime in Known Voices, or delete everything with Forget All Voices.
  • No network: the app is sandboxed without network access, so it cannot send anything anywhere. All AI models are included in the app.
  • No accounts, tracking, analytics, or advertising. Settings such as your preferred view are stored locally by macOS.

Uninstalling Blabacus removes its container, including any voiceprints.

Questions: see Support.
